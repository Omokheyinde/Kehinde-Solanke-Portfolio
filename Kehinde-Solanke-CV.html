Founder | Finclarity Labs - PolicyNova

Aug 2025 - Present · Product Discovery & Testing · AI powered InsurTech project
  • Identified a recurring insurance comprehension problem through conversations with customers, policyholders, friends and family: people often believed they were covered until exclusions or conditions surfaced at the moment of a claim.
  • Used UK regulator, academic and industry evidence to test whether the problem was widespread enough to justify deeper discovery, connecting comprehension gaps to rejected claims, duplicate cover and avoidable consumer loss.
  • Defined target user segments and wrote user stories with explicit measurable success signals so the prototype could test value rather than rely on assumptions.
  • Studied the competitive landscape and precedents including Brolly, Clark and Forgettable, identifying a recurring commercial lesson: policy clarity has user value, but scalable models tend to move closer to insurance distribution.
  • Worked through the core regulatory trade off between helpful information and regulated advice, choosing a comparison and interpretation first MVP with no purchase links while trust and comprehension are tested.
  • Narrowed the first testable wedge to car insurance and prioritised an MVP around onboarding, policy ingestion, plain English interpretation, market comparison, duplicate detection and grounded policy Q&A.
  • Defined six validation measures: trust at decision moments, decision quality, comprehension, repeat usage at high stakes moments, money found through duplicate/gap detection and claim readiness.
Key product learning: The problem is real, but policy interpretation alone may be too episodic to support a scalable business. The next commercial hypothesis would be a broader insurance decision and distribution model, tested only after the core comprehension behaviour is proven.
