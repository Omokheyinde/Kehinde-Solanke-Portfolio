About

The kind of PM I am.

My journey into product started in complaints, where I saw customer frustration firsthand, not as isolated issues, but as signals of where products, journeys and processes were failing people. That experience shaped how I think: listen closely, find the real problem, then ask what we could do better.

I care deeply about discovery. Before thinking about features, I want to understand who we are solving for, what they are trying to achieve and why the problem matters. I use research, product thinking and evidence to turn those insights into clear opportunities and focused product decisions.

I'm drawn to meaningful problems, the kind that require curiosity before solutions. From discovery through delivery and iteration, I bring together user needs, business goals and technical realities to build products with purpose, using frameworks and AI as tools to think, test and learn faster.

What makes me different

Three things I lead with.

01

Curiosity before solutions.

I don't start with the feature. I start by understanding the problem, the people experiencing it, and the evidence behind it, so we solve what actually matters.

02

Evidence over assumptions.

Research, customer insight and data shape my decisions. I test what I think I know, challenge assumptions, and use evidence to decide what deserves to be built.

03

Value through outcomes.

Shipping is not the finish line. I connect user needs, business goals and measurable outcomes, then use what we learn to decide what should happen next.
